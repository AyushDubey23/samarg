Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

SAMARG XI ("SAMARG", "we", "our") operates the realistic cricket drafting and tournament simulation web platform. We respect your privacy and protect data collected when you draft squads or host multiplayer lobbies.

2. Data We Collect

We only collect data strictly necessary to facilitate game operations:

  • Anonymous Authentication UID: Generated by Google Firebase to track your active session and multiplayer room ownership without requiring passwords or emails.
  • Player Display Name: The public handle you choose for rooms and global leaderboards.
  • Gameplay Statistics: Draft picks, match scores, tournament brackets, and Net Run Rate (NRR) calculations.

3. Cloud Infrastructure & Security Rules

Zero Unauthorized Access Guarantee: SAMARG enforces server-side database security rules. Open reading or writing to our database roots is prohibited. Client room updates are permitted exclusively for authenticated sessions.

4. Sub-Processors

  • Google Firebase: Hosts authentication, low-latency Realtime Database, and Cloud Firestore in secure Google Cloud data centers.
  • Google Analytics: (Measurement ID: G-DSSQWVB7N7) Activated only if you grant explicit analytics consent.

5. Contact & Inquiries

For questions or data deletion requests, contact our privacy team at privacy@samarg-cricket.app or inspect our open-source codebase on GitHub.
