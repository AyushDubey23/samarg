# SAMARG Asset Licensing & Sources

To ensure compliance, SAMARG does not use scraped or copyrighted images, prose, or player photos. All assets are self-contained, lightweight SVGs or sourced from permissively licensed open-source packages.

---

## 1. Player Silhouettes

All player headshots and avatars are flat-color SVG silhouettes categorized by role (batter, bowler, wicketkeeper, all-rounder).
- **Source**: Created in-house for the SAMARG project.
- **License**: Creative Commons Zero (CC0) / Public Domain.

---

## 2. Team Flags

Team flags are SVG graphics of national flags used in cricket tournaments.
- **Source**: Sourced from [Flagpack](https://github.com/Yummygum/flagpack) and [flag-icons](https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons).
- **License**: MIT License.

---

## 3. Historical Statistics

Historical cricket statistics (career averages, strike rates, bowling economies) represent factual historical records.
- **Source**: Compiled from public aggregate records (such as ESPN Cricinfo Statsguru and ICC historical databases).
- **Attribution**: Factual statistics are not copyrightable, but names and tournament records are attributed to their respective eras for realism.
