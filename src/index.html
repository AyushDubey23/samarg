Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How does the cricket draft system work?

In each draft turn, a randomized historical tournament squad is revealed. You choose 1 player and place them into an eligible pitch position (Openers, Middle Order, All-Rounders, Bowlers). You can use your team or year rerolls to hunt for legendary tournament champions!

Is SAMARG free to play?

Yes, SAMARG is 100% free with no in-app purchases, microtransactions, or gambling. It is an open sports strategy simulation for cricket enthusiasts worldwide.

Can I play with friends online?

Yes! Click “Create Room” on the homepage, share the 6-character room code with your friend, and compete in a live synchronized draft followed by an interactive match toss and simulated head-to-head clash.
